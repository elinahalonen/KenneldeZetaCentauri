A small kennel with a clear why

The dogs themselves come first

Zeta Centauri is a small Polish Hunting Spaniel kennel in North Holland, the Netherlands, built around a simple idea: the dogs themselves come first.

We breed rarely and deliberately, follow the dogs we breed throughout their lives, and use what we learn to inform the next generation.

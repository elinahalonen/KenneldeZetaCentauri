A small kennel with a clear why

We breed for the dog the puppy becomes

Zeta Centauri is a tiny, deliberate kennel of Polish Hunting Spaniels in the Netherlands. Our focus isn't the trophy wall — it's scent, temperament and a gentle start in life. The sniffing dog in our logo is no accident: nosework is where these dogs come alive, and where we do too.

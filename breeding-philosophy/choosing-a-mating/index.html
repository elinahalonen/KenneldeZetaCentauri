Breeding Philosophy

Choosing a Mating

Breeding is a probabilistic decision, not a recipe. The aim is to understand the dogs and their families well enough to preserve strengths, reduce vulnerabilities and deliberately shift the odds in the next generation.

Planned coverage (draft notes — not final copy):

  • Choose a combination, not an abstract “best stud.”
  • Ask what this female contributes, what she needs, what should be preserved, and what should be balanced.
  • Consider individual health, structure, movement, temperament, working ability and everyday behaviour.
  • Consider parents, siblings, offspring where available, grandparents / wider family and recurring line traits.
  • Use genetic tests, COI, population information and family history as evidence, not destiny.
  • Be explicit about uncertainty and trade-offs.
  • Longitudinal outcomes from prior litters feed into future mating decisions.
  • For Astra × Bueno: preserve Astra's strengths; add robustness / calmness / boldness without flattening working quality.

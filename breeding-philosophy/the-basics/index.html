Breeding Philosophy

The Basics

Zeta Centauri is registered with the Raad van Beheer, the Dutch Kennel Club, and our litters are registered through the Dutch pedigree system. We follow the applicable Raad van Beheer rules and welfare requirements for pedigree breeding.

Registration, identification and parentage

All puppies are microchipped and registered before they leave us.

The Raad van Beheer also requires DNA parentage verification for pedigree puppies. DNA is collected from each puppy and compared with the DNA profiles of the recorded parents, allowing the parentage to be confirmed independently.

What puppies leave with

Before leaving for their new homes, our puppies receive:

  • a veterinary examination;
  • a microchip;
  • DNA parentage verification;
  • registration with the Raad van Beheer;
  • an official pedigree;
  • an EU pet passport;
  • age-appropriate vaccinations;
  • age-appropriate parasite treatment.

We provide the relevant documentation and health information to the puppy's family when they go home.

After your puppy goes home

Our involvement does not end when a puppy leaves Zeta Centauri. We remain available to our families throughout the dog's life for questions, support and discussion, and we continue to share useful new information as our own knowledge develops.

You can read more about this under For Our Puppy Families.

Breeding Philosophy

Why This Breed Matters to Us

I breed Polish Hunting Spaniels because I believe in the vision of the breed, not because I think other spaniels are lesser dogs.

The PSM is a small population and any community effort is inherently imperfect, but there is a serious opportunity to build health, diversity and function into the breed’s future while the population is still developing.

Planned coverage (draft notes — not final copy):

  • Belief in the breed ideal: moderate, functional hunting spaniel, closely bonded with people, capable of real work and real family life.
  • Community vision: careful outcrossing, attention to COI and genetic diversity, health information, responsible development.
  • Historical advantage over older breeds: modern genetic tools and population thinking are available now — not a moral judgement on breeders who worked before those tools existed.
  • Working and show lines in PSMs have not diverged into clearly separate populations; many show champions are also active hunting dogs.
  • Community goal of provisional FCI recognition in the near future.
  • Elina and husband leading development of a new breed health database as a practical contribution.
  • Link across to breed history and breed standard rather than repeating them.

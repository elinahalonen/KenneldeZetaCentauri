Bringing Your Puppy Home

Keep this practical: the first days should be about safety, predictability, relationship and settling into ordinary life, not proving how much training can be accomplished in a week.

Planned coverage (draft notes — not final copy):

  • First days / sleep / routine / safe spaces.
  • Household management and preventing avoidable conflict.
  • Recall foundations and freedom-enabling skills.
  • Handling / veterinary preparation.
  • Introductions to people, dogs, environments and transport at an individual pace.
  • Rest and avoiding overstimulation.
  • When to contact the breeder.

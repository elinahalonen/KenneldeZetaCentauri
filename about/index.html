Our Story

Zeta Centauri did not begin with an ambition to breed dogs. It began with Nell, the dog who changed the direction of our lives and taught us to ask better questions about what dogs need from us.

Over time those questions became more concrete: what can a breeder influence before a puppy ever reaches its family, what passes from one generation to the next, and what would it mean to do our small part well?

I cannot make the future better for every dog. I can give a small number of dogs the best start I know how to give them, try to stack the odds towards minds and bodies that allow them to thrive, and help place them in families where life together is harmonious and joyful.

I am also fascinated by behavioural inheritance: the way persistence, sensitivity, working style, independence, social orientation and other tendencies appear, disappear and recombine across generations. Breeding gives me a rare opportunity to observe that longitudinally, while remembering that inherited tendencies always unfold through development and experience.

I am a Polish Hunting Spaniel breeder because I believe in the vision of this breed. Anything that depends on people coordinating will be imperfect, but the vision is solid: a functional hunting spaniel, bred with serious attention to health, diversity, working ability and the future of a still-small population.

Nell

Nell's official kennel name was Zeta Centauri, "the bright one." She was charming, intense and entirely herself — first spaniel, heart dog, and the namesake of this kennel. She changed how we understood behaviour, agency, trust and the dog's perspective.

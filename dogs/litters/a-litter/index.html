Grace × Hunter

Grace × Hunter produced six dogs: Ambra, Wittra, Artis, Atlas, Alba and Astra. This page is not simply an archive of a litter. It is the story of six puppies becoming six adult dogs, six family matches, and what following them has taught us.

What we learned

Draft notes: some predictions held, some adult traits emerged later, context shapes expression, following dogs longitudinally matters, and contrast between siblings is often more informative than isolated adjectives. Astra carries this learning forward into the planned mating with Bueno.

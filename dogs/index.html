Our Dogs

A family of dogs across generations

The dogs of Zeta Centauri include the dogs who live with us and the dogs we have bred who now live with their own families.

We stay connected with our puppy families throughout the lives of the dogs we raise. We are available for support whenever it is needed, and we share new information and research as our own knowledge develops.

Our families have a shared WhatsApp group, so they can talk directly to one another as well as to us. We share successes and important updates, but also ordinary dog life when the mood strikes: photographs, ridiculous behaviour, training moments and family resemblance.
